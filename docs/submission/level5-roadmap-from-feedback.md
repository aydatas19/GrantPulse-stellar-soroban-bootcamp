## Phase 6 Candidate Roadmap

1. Public grant gallery with search by owner, grant ID, and active status.
2. Reviewer invite URLs that pre-fill owner address and grant ID.
3. Transaction proof verification against Horizon or Stellar Expert before feedback export.
4. Cohort analytics for builder, reviewer, founder, and tester segments.
5. Exportable one-page report for demo days and grant program updates.
